The Quest Card Companion (QCC) is a comprehensive collection of Bunny Bits, instructions, and card rules with clarifications made by fans.

The QCC Addams Family Edition uses colored text to identify sources of text. It is formatted for US Letter-sized paper, but at 219 pages, many fans use their tablets to view it.

Quest Fan Card Companion 2018.07.22

(Updated 22 July 2018)

Download PDF (1.1 MiB)

The Quest Fan Card Companion (QFCC) is a collection of all “Bunny Bits,” instructions, and card rules for the Fan Art cards made by Killer Bunnies Quest fans more creative than you.

This collection only contains cards that are available on MagicCarrot.com. As cards are added or removed, the QFCC is updated to reflect the changes.
