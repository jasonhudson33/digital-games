# Secret Hitler

Secret Hitler owns private roles, elections, the policy deck and executive powers for five to ten players, held on the server.

## Language

**Role**: Liberal, Fascist or Hitler, visible only to the owning seat except where the rules reveal teammates.

**Policy Deck**: 6 Liberal and 11 Fascist policies.

## Relationships

- Fascists and Liberals by table size: 5 = 1/3, 6 = 1/4, 7 = 2/4, 8 = 2/5, 9 = 3/5, 10 = 3/6, plus one Hitler.
- A seat's payload never contains other players' roles.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Does a Liberal's response include the others' roles?"
> **Domain expert:** "No, only their own."
