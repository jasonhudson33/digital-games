# Skull King

Skull King owns ten-round trick bidding, the complete 93-card expansion deck, four custom suits, capture bonuses, dual-value cards, Doubloon alliances, and special-card resolution.

## Language

**Bid**: The exact number of tricks a player predicts they will win in the current round.

**Choice Card**: One bonus-free card in each suit that becomes either 0 or 14 when played.

**Sea Monster**: The Kraken, White Whale, or Spotted Stingray. If several Sea Monsters are played in one trick, only the last one has an effect unless Davy Jones’ Locker destroys them.

**Expansion Number**: The second 7 and 8 in each suit. With an exact bid, a captured expansion 7 subtracts 5 points and an expansion 8 adds 5 points.

**Wild Monkey 15**: A 15 that can represent green, yellow, or purple, but never black. It adopts an established nonblack lead automatically.

**Doubloon Alliance**: A relationship between the player of a Doubloon and the winner of that trick. Each earns 20 bonus points if both make their bids.

**Pirate Ability**: A named Pirate's action, unlocked only when that Pirate wins a trick.

**Tigress**: A special card declared as either a Pirate or an Escape when played. She adopts that role's hierarchy and bonuses for the trick.

## Relationships

- A **Trick** normally belongs to its highest led-suit card, unless black trump or a Pirate wins it.
- A captured standard 14 awards its bonus only when the trick winner also makes their **Bid** exactly.
- Pirates capture Mermaids for 20 bonus points each, the Skull King captures Pirates for 30 each, and a Mermaid captures the Skull King for 40.
- A **Doubloon Alliance** scores only when both linked players make their bids exactly.
- The Kraken destroys its trick; the White Whale makes the highest number win; the Spotted Stingray makes the lowest number win.
- Davy Jones’ Locker destroys every Sea Monster in its trick and can award its player 20 points per destroyed monster.
- Rosie D’ Laney chooses the next leader; Bendt the Bandit exchanges two cards with the undealt deck; Rascal of Roatan wagers on the bid; Juanita Jade inspects the undealt deck; Harry the Giant adjusts the bid by one; Mary Thorne forces a random card from a chosen player into the next trick.
- First Mate Con beats Pirates, loses to Mermaids and the Skull King, and can use each captured named Pirate's ability when he wins.
- Walk the Plank removes one Pirate before the trick is resolved. The Last Volley grants its player one extra play, after which that player skips the final trick.

## Deck inventory

- Four suits with standard ranks 1–14, one choice 0/14, and duplicate expansion 7 and 8 cards: 68 cards.
- Wild Monkey 15: 1 card.
- Escapes: 5 cards. Doubloons: 2 cards.
- Mermaids: 2 cards. Named Pirates: 6 cards.
- Tigress, Skull King, First Mate Con, Kraken, White Whale, Spotted Stingray, Walk the Plank, The Last Volley, and Davy Jones’ Locker: 1 each.

The Eel is not part of the deck; the Spotted Stingray is the lowest-number-wins Sea Monster.
