# Hearts

Hearts owns trick-taking, passing, damage cards, and classic or Killer scoring.

## Language

**Trick**: One card played by each active player under follow-suit rules.

**Damage Card**: A heart or queen of spades that contributes penalty points.

**Killer Hearts**: The multi-deck variant for five to eight players.

## Relationships

- A **Trick** awards its **Damage Cards** to its winner.
- **Killer Hearts** can cancel matching lead-suit cards when determining a winner.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Can a **Damage Card** be discarded on the first trick?"
> **Domain expert:** "No, first-trick damage is prohibited."
