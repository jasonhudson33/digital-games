# Dominion

Dominion owns the shared supply, deck cycling, action and buy turns, and end-of-game scoring for two to six players.

## Language

**Supply**: The shared piles of treasure, victory, curse and kingdom cards.

**Kingdom**: The ten action cards in play this game.

**Cleanup**: Discarding hand and played cards, then drawing five.

## Relationships

- Estates and Duchies start at 8 each with two players and 12 with more; Provinces are 8 (two players), 12 (three or four), 15 (five) or 18 (six).
- The game ends after a turn when the Province pile is empty, or three piles (four with five or six players) are empty.
- Victory values are Estate 1, Duchy 3, Province 6, Curse −1; ties go to the player with fewer turns, and remaining ties share the win.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Two players tie on points. Who wins?"
> **Domain expert:** "The one who took fewer turns; equal turns share it."
