# Flip 7

Flip 7 owns the press-your-luck draw loop, busting, action and modifier cards, and first-to-200 scoring for two to ten players.

## Language

**Bust**: Flipping a number already held, scoring nothing for the round.

**Second Chance**: An action card that cancels one duplicate instead of busting.

**Flip 7**: Seven unique number cards, which ends the round with a 15-point bonus.

## Relationships

- A round ends when every player has stayed or busted, or when someone flips seven unique numbers.
- Round score is the number total (doubled by x2) plus plus-modifiers.
- The game ends when exactly one player holds the high score at or above 200; a tie at the top plays another round.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Two players tie at 210?"
> **Domain expert:** "Another round settles it."
